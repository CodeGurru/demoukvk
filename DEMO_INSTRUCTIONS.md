## 🎙️ 3-Minute Judge Demo Script (Step-by-Step)

### 1. (0:00 - 0:30) Samasya (Problem Statement)
> *"Namaste Judges! Bharat ke Tier-2 aur Tier-3 shehron (jaise Ayodhya) me jab AC kharab hota hai, toh do badi dikkat aati hain:
> 1. Customer ko lootne ka dar rehta hai ki technician jhooth bolkar faltu gas ya PCB ka kharcha bata dega.
> 2. Ground-level technician trained nahi hote, wo bina safety check kiye aur bina vacuum kiye seedha gas daal dete hain, jisse compressor blast ya customer loss hota hai.
> Iska samadhan hai: **AI Supervisor by Bijli Wala Aya**."*

### 2. (0:30 - 1:15) AI Voice Call & Camera Vision
- **Live Call dikhayein:** Upar AI Avatar ring pulse kar raha hai aur waveform chalu hai.
- **Hindi me bolkar sunayein:** 
  - `🎤 बोलें` button dabayein ya `❄️ ठंडा नहीं कर रहा` chip par click karein.
  - AI turant Hindi me bolkar samjhata hai.
- **AI Camera Vision ("Dekho"):**
  - `📹 कैमरा ऑन करें` par click karein.
  - `🔄 एंगल बदलें` se Back / Front camera switch karke dikhayein.
  - `⚡ फोटो स्कैन करें` dabayein -> AI ne turant nameplate scan karke **LG 1.5 Ton Inverter, R32 Gas, 6.5A Rated Current** auto-detect kar liya!

### 3. (1:15 - 2:00) Physics Rules Engine & "Sample Dekho Aur Karo" (Hero Feature)
- **Visual Gauge Dial (PSI):**
  - Manifold Gauge ka realistic SVG dial dikhayein.
  - Suction reading `40 PSI` dalein -> Needle ghumeegi aur Red Alert aayega: **"गैस बहुत कम है (Low Gas / Leakage suspected)"**.
- **Mandatory Safety Gate (Suraksha):**
  - Dikhayein ki jab tak MCB off, Capacitor discharge aur Rubber gloves tick nahi hote, tab tak repair **LOCKED 🔒** rehta hai.
- **Supervisor ka Antim Faisla (Physics & Safety Locks):**
  - 88% Confidence score dikhayein.
  - Sabse bada differentiator dikhayein: **"सीधी गैस भरना सख्त मना है (LOCKED 🔒) — pehle leak test aur vacuum zaroori hai!"**

### 4. (2:00 - 2:30) Official Wiring Diagram & Transparent Quotation
- **Official Wiring Diagram:** Verified knowledge base se colour-coded schematic (Compressor UVW, PCB, Fan motor).
- **Itemized Bill:** Sab kuch transparent (Inspection ₹199, Leak Fix ₹350, Vacuum ₹250, Genuine Gas ₹600 = ₹1,399).
- **OTP Closure:** Customer ka OTP `4829` daalkar job close karein aur 45 din ki guarantee shuru!

### 5. (2:30 - 3:00) Business Vision & Why We Win
> *"Hamare paas Bijli Wala Aya ka ground data aur real customer base hai. Ye sirf ek chatbot nahi hai, ye technicians ke liye Senior Supervisor hai jo loot aur accidents ko 0% par lata hai. Hum ise har appliance aur IoT hardware sensors tak le ja rahe hain. Thank you!"*
